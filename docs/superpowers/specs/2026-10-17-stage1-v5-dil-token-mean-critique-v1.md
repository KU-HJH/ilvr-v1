## Macrothink spread, divergence first

or compatibility implementation to three same-model fresh (`fork_turns=none`)
read-only agents. They could inspect source but could not edit, train, invoke
advisory hooks or spawn. All three returned completed independent reads.

No read disproves equal-token normalization for the current `selection=all`
scope. The strongest conditional incompatible finding is that an expanded
`intersection` mode cannot assume metadata counts equal selected counts.
That expanded scope was not proposed. Do not relabel the warning as proof
of the restricted implementation's correctness.

`divergent-compatible` — `/root/token_mean_fresh_a` and

`Fraction(str(percent))/100`. Supporting those three does not inherently
need extra model passes. The commitment retains only current `all` scope
as an explicit scope choice, not a mathematical impossibility claim.

`convergent` — `/root/token_mean_fresh_c` independently favored the smallest
current all-selector scope, separate DIL scale, saved reduction identity,

Both other reads also independently suggested the same sum/token-count

## Additional controller source finding and final synthesis

`CustomTrainerStage1V5.train` compares the entire effective saved/runtime
contract, including source hashes. `test_resume.py` explicitly preserves
source identity as semantics. Thus accepting historical sidecar parsing does
not establish that a historical run may resume under changed source.

Do not add a legacy execution framework or relax these guards. The final
commitment simplifies the proposal: new training uses token mean under a new
sidecar schema/reduction identity; historical schema 1 remains readable for
inspection, but training resume in the new implementation rejects it before
model work. Resume the historical objective using its original reviewed
revision. This preserves historical artifacts and standing policy allowing
exact represented semantics or rejection before training. Changing the
objective always requires a fresh run.

Remaining uncertainty: no new implementation, mathematical dispatch test,
GPU backward or two-node training has run. No performance or generation
improvement is claimed. Independent implementation review and fresh tests
remain gates after final human approval.
